How well each day is forecast

Mean absolute error of the hourly forecast in percent of the actual load, 2023 to 2025, on the days both forecasts scored. Each cell reads recipe / DF: the open day-ahead forecaster of this initiative, built from public load and public weather forecasts at a 9 a.m. cut-off the day before, against the forecast each BA itself reports to the EIA. Underlined where the recipe is better. Click a column header to sort.

Source: hourly demand of Form EIA-930 (U.S. Energy Information Administration), on the days the forecasting study scored (days with reporting faults left out). Seasons: winter December to February, summer June to August, shoulder the other months. Holidays are the special days the forecasting study marks (the main federal holidays and the eves and bridge days around them); weekdays, Saturdays and Sundays exclude them. Percent scale divides each hour by the mean load of the same group of days. Part of Cardinal Grid, an independent open initiative.
