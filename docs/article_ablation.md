# Main article: the recipe with one piece taken out at a time

Development BAs, 2023-01-01 to 2025-12-31, protocol v2, the days every model scored. `final10s` is the recipe; each `abl_*` takes one piece out and refits: special-day labels (`abl_nospec`), the extra feature blocks of load, temperature and humidity (`abl_noextra`), the third member (`abl_norec`), the error-weighted ensemble (`abl_mean`, equal weights), six years of training (`abl_2y`, two), the NBM and GFS in the temperature (`abl_hrrr`, HRRR alone, band without the spread factor) and the extra stations (`abl_1st`, the first station of each BA). `*_ensemble` is each one before the correction from the 40 nearest known days.
