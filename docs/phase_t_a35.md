# Phase T: the members against their shrinkage at the peak (A35)

Development BAs: BPAT, CISO, DUK, ERCO, FPL, ISNE, MISO, NYIS, PJM, SWPP, TVA, 2020-12-02 to 2022-12-31, 9 a.m. cut-off, HRRR temperature at several stations per BA, six years of training. Only BA-days on which every model produced a forecast are counted; documented load-shed days are left out. Protocol v2 (`docs/phase_g_protocol.md`). Three variants of `final10s`, each refitting its members: the days whose observed daily peak is above the 80th percentile of the training days weigh 1 + (excess / 5 %) in both members (`a35w`), the same weight on the hours within 3 h of the day's peak only (`a35h`), and the boosting fitted to the 55th percentile instead of the mean (`a35q`).
