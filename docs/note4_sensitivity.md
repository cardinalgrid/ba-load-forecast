# Note 4, journal version: sensitivity of the bands and probabilistic baselines

Development BAs: BPAT, CISO, DUK, ERCO, FPL, ISNE, MISO, NYIS, PJM, SWPP, TVA, 2020-12-02 to 2022-12-31, 9 a.m. cut-off, HRRR temperature at several stations per BA, six years of training. Only BA-days on which every model produced a forecast are counted; documented load-shed days are left out. Protocol v2 (`docs/phase_g_protocol.md`). Bands on the shared ensemble of `final10s`: the spread exponent 0.5 and 2 (`n4_spread05`, `n4_spread20`), wider bounds 0.5-3 (`n4_bounds_wide`), no spread factor (`n4_nospread`, the band of phase N), the cold-day level and tail (`n4_a2t`) with the level scale 0.5 and 0.1 and the cold decile 5 % and 20 %, a plain adaptive interval without regime or nearest days (`n4_aci_plain`), fixed empirical quantiles of the last 60 days (`n4_emp60`), and a quantile gradient boosting at 5, 50 and 95 % (`n4_qboost`, its own model, rerun as group `n4_qb` so that its interval is scored; three quantiles give no CRPS).
